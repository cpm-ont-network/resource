Abstract

The Construction Resources (CR) Ontology provides a semantic model for representing construction resources and their relationships with cost items, tasks, geometry, and resource assignments. It distinguishes material, equipment, and labour resources and supports the representation of quantities, costs, consumption, productivity, and assignment information in construction processes.

Introduction back to ToC

Construction resources are fundamental production factors in construction processes and must be consistently related to activities, cost items, quantities, and geometrical information. The Construction Resources (CR) Ontology provides a structured representation of material, equipment, and labour resources and supports their assignment to construction tasks and cost information. CR is designed to interoperate with the Cost Item (CI) and Construction Terminology (CTERM) ontologies and includes alignments with Digital Twin Construction (DTC) concepts.

The Construction Resources (CR) Ontology: Description back to ToC

The Construction Resources (CR) Ontology is based on concepts and principles of construction project management to provide a centralized definition of resources that can be used as shared entities with task planning and cost estimation for providing a unified context.
